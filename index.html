Hey, I'm Rakeh.

I'm a Data Scientist at GA Telesis, where I build demand-forecasting and Document AI systems — transformer and gradient-boosting pipelines running in production on AWS.

Before industry, I earned my PhD at Penn State, teaching machines to see the way expert inspectors do — using eye tracking, drones, and deep learning to assess buildings and bridges. That research thread is still very much alive.

If you're working on hard problems where models have to survive contact with the physical world, let's talk.
